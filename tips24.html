To make your computer work fast, you can clear junk files, turn off unnecessary startup programs, and change your performance settings. ** Clear Junk Files** Press the Windows Key + R on your keyboard to open the Run box. Type %temp% and press Enter to open the temporary folder. Select all the files inside the folder and delete them to free up space. Search for Disk Cleanup in your Windows search bar and run it on your main drive. Disable Startup Apps Press Control + Shift + Escape to open the Task Manager. Click on the Startup apps tab on the left side. Right-click any app you do not need when your computer starts and choose Disable. You can read more performance tips on Microsoft Support Adjust Visual Effects Open your start menu, search for Adjust the appearance and performance of Windows, and open it. Select the option for Adjust for best performance under the Visual Effects tab. Click Apply and then OK to save the changes. Upgrade Hardware Switch your old hard drive to a Solid-State Drive (SSD) for much faster boot times. Add more Random Access Memory (RAM) if your computer runs out of memory while opening many apps.
